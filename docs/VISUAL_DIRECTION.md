# CSE Under Box Visual Direction

CSE Under Box will use the visual language established by Just Ordered, adapted for learning.

## Shared visual principles

- Premium, clean, spacious interface
- Light background with white elevated cards
- Strong blue primary action color
- Soft borders and restrained shadows
- Rounded cards and generous spacing
- Clear typography hierarchy
- Friendly, approachable product feel
- Responsive layouts for desktop and smaller screens
- Empty states and status text that do not depend only on color

## Education-specific adaptation

Just Ordered uses shopping categories and products. CSE Under Box will use:

- Years and terms instead of shopping categories
- Courses instead of products
- Lessons instead of product details
- Labs and projects instead of orders
- Learning progress instead of wallet balance
- Resource library instead of collection
- Downloaded course packages instead of purchased items

## Design boundary

The visual style is inspired by our own Just Ordered product language, but CSE Under Box will have its own identity, iconography, educational labels, and information architecture.
